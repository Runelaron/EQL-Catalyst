# Changes

## Beta 1.1 — 1.1.0-beta.1

- Repair table and workspace splitters with nearest-first cascading, visible panel handles, bounded widths, and stable saved layouts.
- Keep tab hover marks inside unselected headers; selected pages and their dropdowns no longer trigger them.
- Add the right-aligned character/server selector to Tracker, switching the whole app while protecting unsaved Theorycraft work and separate profiles.
- Simplify Gear expansion labels and single-expansion summaries.
- Remove redundant inspector spacing. Add alphabetical visual Damage emblem and Tab style selectors with selected previews, and place the live meter example below its selector.
- Add idle cutoff presets with an explicit Custom field, clearer timing labels and unchanged timing calculations.
- Keep Display setup and Hotkeys expanded. Add per-character customizable overlay shortcuts, conflict-safe registration and updated recovery hints.
- Deepen Shadowman into a neutral near-black theme while retaining transparency and semantic colors.
- Refresh Windows packages, synthetic screenshots, privacy/secret/dependency checks and artifact-specific validation.

Profiles and saved builds remain compatible. The installer remains unsigned. Physical-monitor DPI, live-game input/capture and disputed game mechanics remain separate acceptance items.

## Beta 1.0 — 1.0.0-beta.1

First public beta of the local-first EverQuest Legends companion.

- Compact DPS/healing overlays, independent filters, Combat Lab, sessions/history/exports and Group/Raid contributions.
- Quest queue, item/quest discovery, walkthroughs and reversible progress.
- Gear and Pet builds, classic item details, rank previews, explained preference ratings, Undo and isolated Theorycraft.
- Exaltation compatibility, ownership evidence and content filtering.
- Coldain, Sunstrider, Shadowman and Auto themes; Clean/Vibrant artwork and appearance controls.
- Unsigned, self-contained Windows x64 wizard installer with optional all-users scope, offline first launch and data-preserving uninstall.
- Neutral startup facts, synthetic fixtures/screenshots, path-mapped builds and a maintained GPL-3.0-only source release.

Known limits: physical-monitor DPI and live-game input/capture acceptance remain pending. Some mechanics and roster/effect coverage remain explicitly unresolved. Class Loadout comparison is in development. Preference points are not predicted DPS.
