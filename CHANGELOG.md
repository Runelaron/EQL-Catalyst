## Beta 1.0 — 1.0.0-beta.1

First public beta of the local-first EverQuest Legends companion.

- Compact DPS/healing overlays, independent filters, Combat Lab, sessions/history/exports and Group/Raid contributions.
- Quest queue, item/quest discovery, walkthroughs and reversible progress.
- Gear and Pet builds, classic item details, rank previews, explained preference ratings, Undo and isolated Theorycraft.
- Exaltation compatibility, ownership evidence and content filtering.
- Coldain, Sunstrider, Shadowman and Auto themes; Clean/Vibrant artwork and appearance controls.
- Unsigned, self-contained Windows x64 wizard installer with optional all-users scope, offline first launch and data-preserving uninstall.
- Neutral startup facts, synthetic fixtures/screenshots, path-mapped builds and a maintained GPL-3.0-only source release.

Known limits: physical-monitor DPI and live-game input/capture acceptance remain pending. Some mechanics and roster/effect coverage remain explicitly unresolved. Class Loadout comparison is in development. Preference points are not predicted DPS.
