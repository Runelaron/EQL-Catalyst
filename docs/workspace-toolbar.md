# Shared workspace toolbar

The 28-DIP toolbar carries the gold EQL Catalyst emblem, Tracker, Combat Lab and Settings. A verified ready update appears immediately to Settings' right. Your separately chosen application and tray icon stays unchanged.

Click Tracker or Combat Lab to open its attached panel. Click the selected button again to return to the bar, or click the other workspace to switch. Outside clicks leave the workspace selected. The existing hide-when-unfocused preference still controls temporary visibility. Tray commands explicitly open the requested workspace.

Drag empty toolbar space to move the bar and attached workspace. Double-click empty toolbar space to toggle the last workspace. The panel header's collapse action returns to the bar; its header no longer moves the panel independently. The right-hand toolbar grip changes width only. Panel grips retain each workspace's expanded dimensions independently. Lock toolbar is available from the bar's context menu.

Drag Tracker or Combat Lab over the other button to reorder them. Right-click either button and choose Move left or Move right, or focus it and press Alt+Left/Right. Reordering does not activate the moved button. The selected workspace remains pressed.

Toolbar placement, width, order and open state are saved separately for each character. Existing Tracker and Combat Lab layout records retain their expanded sizes and pane proportions. Panels prefer the space below the bar, use available space above when needed, and recover into the monitor work area. A temporary fit on a smaller monitor does not replace saved expanded dimensions. Completed panel resizes deliberately save a new expanded size.

Existing Save / Discard / Cancel prompts protect drafts when switching or hiding a workspace or exiting the application. Cancel retains the current workspace. DPS selectors and overlays remain independent.

## Translucent presentation and pet artwork

The bar now shares the workspace's translucent theme surface and exposed outer frame, with dark flat button faces. Its own width remains independent. The branded workspace heading starts 6 DIP from the joined edge. Fast fade/slide transitions respect Windows and app motion preferences; resize and visibility recovery snap immediately. [Presentation help](translucent-art.md) explains the behavior.

The approved defaults are Water C, Fire Ifrit B, Monster D and Skeleton A. Settings → Appearance also offers Wraith A and Wraith B for Undead, saved per character. Open [the current review gallery](previews/pet-art-r3-review.html) for 18 class-inspired concepts and archived earlier rounds. Sizes are 13, 14, 16, 20, 24, 32 and 64 DIP on all three theme backgrounds, including actual synthetic DPS selector captures, silhouettes and grayscale. Clean retains simplified vectors. Vibrant uses matching simplified artwork through 24 DIP, then painted detail decoded for the monitor's pixel density. Artwork selection does not change pet identity.

This local iteration does not change combat ingestion, research measurements, ranks, equipment, independently saved log checkpoints, public release artifacts or installers.
