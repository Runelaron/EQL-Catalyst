# Pet appearance: Classic and Classes

Settings → Appearance saves each elemental family's appearance independently for the selected character. Air defaults to Classes — Monk, Water to Classes — Rogue, and Fire to Classes — Wizard staff. The existing Fire crystal preference is preserved. Classic restores the original elemental artwork; Water also offers Elemental torrent. Earth remains Classic until a class-inspired treatment is approved.

Clean and Vibrant remain separate choices. Vibrant uses the original approved paintings at every size, including 13–24 DIP, decoded for the display's pixel density. Clean keeps simplified vectors at every size. Changing appearance refreshes DPS controls, summaries, comparison cards and Pet Details without changing detected identity, historical associations or either independent DPS selector.

The compact-mane attacking lion represents Warder. The floating sword and shield represents the Enchanter Animation family. Archived Monster A is the Monster default. Skeleton A, Wraith A and Wraith B remain available for Undead.

The [round-five gallery](previews/pet-art-r5-review.html) contains four Dragon and four Aviak double-axe berserkers. Settings → Appearance → Monster appearance now offers the selected interim alternatives **Aviak C — Overhead**, **Dragon C — Overhead**, **Aviak B — Diagonal** and **Dragon B — Diagonal**, alongside **Monster A (default)**. Their existing paintings and simplified vectors are used unchanged; axe shaft straightness remains a pending artwork refinement. Selecting one changes appearance only and preserves the other families' choices.

The remaining four concepts stay review-only. Each concept has painted and simplified treatments, grayscale and silhouette comparisons, native DPS control samples, and prompt/reference/output hashes in the [provenance manifest](previews/pet-icon-candidates/manifest.json). Gallery shortlisting changes no saved application setting.
