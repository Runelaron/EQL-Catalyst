# Pet Exaltations

Inventory's **Options** menu contains **Edit selected** and **Remove selected**. Double-click an item's name or Exalt cell to edit that numbered position. Effects stay with the position when equipment moves; intrinsic item facts follow the item.

Only explicitly selected or recorded Exaltations are used for Pet equipment. Catalog intrinsic effects remain reference information, labeled **Catalog effect (not selected)**. Choose one in the editor to use it. Existing selected assignments and effects confirmed by exports are preserved; changing this policy does not record a removal or physical transfer.

Weapons show populated effects in Proc, Focus, Click, Worn order, followed by other configured types. Armor uses Focus, Click, Worn, Proc order. The Exalt column and its full tooltip share the same projection; empty categories are omitted. Planned, rank-locked, inactive and recorded states remain visible.

Each recognized type allows one configured Exaltation, including Ornamentation. Save rejects duplicate types with an explanation. Repeated identical observations do not append duplicates. Older conflicting records are retained for review, with every assignment of that type inactive until you remove the duplicate or explicitly replace that type. Other compatible types remain usable. Review **Assigned effects** in **Edit selected**; no duplicate is silently chosen or deleted.

Rank, class, slot and activation restrictions still apply. Turning Dual wield off or equipping a two-handed Primary leaves dormant Secondary configuration intact. Undo restores the full configuration. Preview effects and plans are not ownership evidence. Pet preference points remain separate from predicted damage; Self and combat calculations are unchanged.
