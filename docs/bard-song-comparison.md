# Bard song comparison

Open **Combat Lab → Loadout Comparison → Songs**. The existing **Song coverage** action takes you to the same view with its group context. Combat samples remain available beside Songs.

The left side shows up to five songs; the right side shows **Keep**, a supported **Swap**, useful alternatives or a specific evidence limitation. **Why?** contains the rank, effect and modifier reasoning. **Setup** holds known-song corrections, Keep choices, Aura capacity and advanced evidence review. Changes here affect Catalyst, not EQ’s spell bar.

## Detected and common sets

**Detected** follows a changed set only after at least three complete supported repeat cycles. Cadence comes from observed repetition. Interruptions, ambiguous casters and another Bard’s songs are not attributed to you. Gem numbers remain unknown when only timing was observed.

Successful memorization establishes a known song. An explicit upgrade suffix records its rank; a bare name leaves rank Unknown, rather than assuming +0. Saved character spell loadouts preserve gem positions, but their `inuse` flags mean saved, not currently playing. Selecting **EQ · …** or a common set pins it for comparison while detection continues. Choose **Detected** to resume following. Planning a swap still requires changing songs manually in EQ.

When gem order is known, selection scans backward from the final gem, skipping ineligible songs. The user-confirmed rule accepts zero-mana, zero-cooldown area/group or Self songs; unknown metadata stays unresolved. Confirmed Aura rank limits capacity. When capacity is unknown, proposals preserve the observed count, never assume five are granted. The [Bard reference](https://eqlwiki.com/Bard#Symphonic_Aura) uses narrower area-only wording; that disagreement remains recorded.

Common sets retain recurrence, episodes, sessions, ranks and bounded observed duration. Missing duration or silent gaps are not credited as uptime. These are familiar combinations, not a complete live buff roster.

## Group strength and rank

Higher upgrade ranks take precedence within the same supported overlap group. A stronger modifier does not revive a blocked lower-rank effect. Unrelated songs are not ranked against one another by their numerals.

**Effective strength** starts checked and is remembered per character. It includes supported gains from singing, instrument focus, confirmed AAs and Amplification. Unchecked retains overlap/rank and missing-coverage recommendations without the additional strength optimization. The entire proposed set is checked so multiple swaps cannot duplicate a benefit or silently remove a useful secondary component. Damage pulses are not treated as redundant persistent buffs.

Known AA ranks and relevant effects on worn gear are displayed automatically. Ownership alone does not activate an instrument or focus. Singing Mastery, Instrument Mastery, Jam Fest, spell rank and caster level remain distinct. Documented AA percentages alone do not prove how multiple bonuses combine. Exact adjusted song components take precedence; reviewed final multipliers apply only to their equipment/AA context. Blank is Unknown, not zero. Verses of Victory’s existing local rank estimates stay scoped and labeled; its haste is not multiplied by an instrument bonus.

Amplification is a candidate only when known and when rank-matched combined Singing evidence supports a net gain after its slot cost. Its [wiki page](https://eqlwiki.com/Amplification) has conflicting overview/effect descriptions. No universal flat multiplier, rank formula or stacking rule is assumed.

Current and historical group contexts remain separate. Old landings need supported durations and recipients; historical comparisons do not borrow today’s equipment or AAs. Missing inputs explain the limitation instead of inventing a winner.

All personal spell-set names, memorization evidence, rotations and modifier captures are stored per character outside bundled catalogs. The Bard metadata reader has its own checkpoint and never replays combat ingestion.
