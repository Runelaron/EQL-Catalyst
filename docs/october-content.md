# October content and mechanics

The app includes offline facts for Lake Nerius, Nektropos Castle and Nektropos Undercroft. The October 20 official announcement establishes the areas and Ayr'Haeth race. Their placement under the app's Classic filter is a current-era inference. It does not establish every item's expansion or fabricate quest rewards.

Race aliases include Ayr’Haeth, Ayr'Haeth, Half Dark Elf and Half Dark Elves. Race level is an optional separate character fact, not class level. Racial ability descriptions are reference information; no extra modifier is applied to observed healing, damage or pet totals. Half Dark Elf equipment access is not inherited from Dark Elf or Half Elf. /who may establish a recognized race, but does not establish race level.

Monster is a distinct Pet family with Warrior/Berserker equipment access plus the existing owner-class route. Monster Summoning I is MAG 30, documented base pet level 29; II is MAG 50, base level 42. Those August wiki references do not supply current HP/mana baselines. The icon combines existing painted Berserker and Pet artwork in Vibrant mode, with a horned face/crossed axes in Clean mode; it does not imply a fixed monster appearance.

Pet mechanics use an October 22 version. Current comparison identities include race and race level, summon/equipment/buff state and mechanics versions. Old benchmarks remain available as history and do not match a current mechanics identity. New benchmarks require explicit patch confirmation; older encounters cannot be relabeled by that checkbox. The adopted 10% item-damage preview model and exact item/rank observations remain unchanged.

The October 22 source documents STA-dependent pet HP, INT/WIS-dependent mana, weapon-preference corrections, the exactly-28-delay damage-bonus correction, a 6-second command hold, threat modifiers (+75% taunt on, −50% off), backstab-capable pets also kicking/bashing, Primary-ratio-dependent Frenzy, Rampage rank 2 at level 40 for WAR/BER, and stance changes. Unpublished coefficients remain unresolved. Gear HP and equipment preferences remain separate from total HP and predicted DPS.

See `pet-measurement-guide.md` and `measurement-template.json` for controlled tests and the supported export/log/client-file collection routes. Client spell parsing records the content hash and rejects unsupported formats; client-derived values alone do not establish server behavior.
