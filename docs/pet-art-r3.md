# Approved pet artwork and class-inspired concepts

The current defaults are Water C, Fire Ifrit B, Monster D and Skeleton A. Their original approved paintings and alpha are preserved without regeneration or pixel editing. Clean uses matching simplified vectors. Vibrant uses simplified artwork through 24 DIP, then painted detail at larger sizes, decoded for the monitor's pixel density.

Settings → Appearance → Undead appearance offers Skeleton A, Wraith A and Wraith B, with 24/40-DIP visual previews. The choice applies immediately and saves per character. Missing or unsupported preferences use Skeleton A. Changing artwork does not change detected pet identity, summon context, historical associations, research, equipment or either independent DPS selection. An unresolved pet still uses the generic fallback. Appearance choices do not indicate an equipped weapon.

Open [the round-three review](previews/pet-art-r3-review.html) for 18 new review-only concepts: three Air monks, three Water rogues, three Fire wizards, three dragon berserkers, three Aviak Monster alternatives and three attacking lion Warders. Elemental bodies remain wind, water and fire; class cues come from pose and broad shapes. Aviak is a Monster artwork alternative, not a new pet family.

Approved defaults and Settings alternatives are labeled. Earlier unselected concepts remain archived. Every new concept has painted and simplified treatments at 13, 14, 16, 20, 24, 32 and 64 DIP, across Coldain, Sunstrider and Shadowman backgrounds, with grayscale, silhouettes and actual synthetic DPS controls. Gallery shortlisting is a review aid and does not update the app.

Prompts and SHA-256 provenance are in [the artwork manifest](previews/pet-icon-candidates/manifest.json). The built-in image-generation tool created the paintings; the small treatments are native vector geometry shared with SVG review files. Personal game captures and generation-machine paths are excluded.

Other production families, application/tray icons, combat calculations, published releases, installers and Store submission are unchanged. Broad window theme/DPI reviews remain deferred.
