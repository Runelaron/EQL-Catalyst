# Connected spell evidence

The existing Ability Details inspector now includes **Spell, buff & focus evidence**. Stat-profile Game evidence includes received buff history, and Pet Details feeds its known abilities from the same owned-pet intervals. No separate spell browser or game-input capture is added.

An internal versioned index connects cached wiki references, summon aliases and validated read-only client spell parameters. Exact catalog names resolve before upgrade suffixes: `Burnout III II` is Burnout III at upgrade rank 2, while `Monster Summoning II VIII` is spell II at upgrade rank 8. Unknown ranks stay unknown. A base reference does not establish upgraded numerical power. Supported static direct-heal parameters remain distinct from measured healing and caster modifiers; unsupported client formulas stay unresolved.

The per-character evidence cache keeps raw/canonical identities, upgrade ranks, caster/recipient evidence, timestamps, candidate spells and landing/fade transitions. It retains the newest 4,000 entries and displays up to 30 matching received transitions. Metadata backfill uses the existing bounded log reader with its own resumable checkpoint, preserving log-generation identities without rewinding shared readers, replaying combat ingestion or modifying private research. Encounter boundaries and rate denominators are unchanged. Repeated observations are idempotent. Missing client message files preserve cached history and do not cause a repeated full spell-file parse.

Cast attempts, failed/interrupted casts, landings and fades remain distinct. A unique uninterrupted cast within 30 seconds can supply correlated caster/rank evidence; it does not prove a complete current buff roster. Ambiguous client messages retain candidates and their original evidence. Missing fades, durations and transitions stay unresolved. Pet abilities and buff history exclude unrelated actors and respect time-ranged ownership, family and summon context, including reused names. Planned sheets do not borrow the latest unrelated pet's buff history.

Focus explanations use the effective **caster's** current equipment. Item ownership alone does not activate a focus. Pet catalog intrinsic effects require explicit configuration; rank locks, conflicting assignments and inactive equipment are respected. Pet equipment does not modify the owner's healing. A foreign or mixed caster whose gear is unavailable receives no inferred loadout. Current equipment is labeled as a reference and is never asserted to have been worn in a historical encounter.

[Improved Healing III](https://eqlwiki.com/Improved_Healing_III), revision 149780, documents a **1–20%** range on qualifying beneficial direct HP heals. It excludes HoTs, Complete Heal, Promised Renewal, percentage heals and combat skills. Its maximum spell level is 60, with 5% effectiveness loss per additional spell level. A reference range is shown only when validated static healing, caster class/level and restrictions are sufficient. Unknown spell-rank power, focus-rank scaling and multiple-source stacking remain unresolved. The range excludes crits and other modifiers and is separate from actual restored HP. A mismatched observed delta cannot prove a focus effect.

Personal log transitions, client-file hashes, screenshots and research remain in local per-character storage. Public builds keep private research disabled. There are no uploads, client writes or new automatic game inputs.
