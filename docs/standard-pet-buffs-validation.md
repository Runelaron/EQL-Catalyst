# Standard pet buffs — focused validation

This local iteration adds an independent character-private standard-buff preset and presentation projection. Inventory, custom weights, equipment preference scores and encounter arithmetic remain separate.

The repair resolves canonical spell identity before interpreting upgrade suffixes. A unique uninterrupted compatible cast can disambiguate a shared landing message. Pending summons survive delayed ownership and ordinary restart, while failures, pet loss, superseding casts and ownership boundaries prevent association.

Historical corrections use metadata-only reading and revisioned research reprojection. The combat reader, shared stat reader and their checkpoints are not rewound. Corrected evidence content participates in research cache identities, including later fades that bound an earlier interval. A confirmed later fade is retained when it disagrees with a nominal duration.

## Focused checks

- 15 standard-buff core checks: confirmed learning, exclusions, rank overrides/restart, unknown/legacy ranks, exact arithmetic, inspected-field precedence, interrupted/ambiguous casts, idempotent corrections, delayed ownership, unrelated actors, paired snapshots, unresolved baselines/stacking, caps and conserved phases.
- 235 Pet-related regressions and 28 spell-related regressions pass. These groups overlap; they are not a unique combined test count.
- 11 synthetic popup checks cover defaults, paired values, independent Added behavior, cap hints, normal/minimum widths, scoped estimates and obsolete-request rejection.
- A private copy of retained evidence reproduces the reviewed three-phase interval, preserves every damage contribution and all encounter seconds, and learns ordinary Burnout without selecting Frenzied.

The normal and minimum-width screenshots use synthetic identities and values. Broad theme/DPI matrices remain deferred. The popup uses one outer scroll surface and retains its fixed Close action.

## Deliberate limits

The rank fit applies only to Burnout III and the inspected Verses of Victory values. Other effects and unverified simultaneous stacking remain unresolved. Raw spell AC is not substituted for displayed pet AC. The paired display deltas apply to their reviewed captured context only. Gear changes do not recalculate an old observation into new totals.

Cap hints do not alter preference ratings. Unknown innate resists, resource conversion coefficients, AA purchase ranks and proc/attack timing remain unknown. Burst effects are historical evidence, not part of standard sustained assumptions.

Local delivery requires privacy scans, verified app/profile/research backups and a graceful restart. Post-update receipts are stored outside the published payload. Published Beta 1.4, installers and Store submission remain unchanged.
