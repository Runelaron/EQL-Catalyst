# Charged click effects

Exaltations hides explicitly charged click effects by default, including descriptions marked **Charge Clicky**. Ordinary click effects with unlimited or unrecorded charges remain visible.

To browse charged candidates, open **Exaltations → Expansion & compatibility → Include charged clicks**. This browsing option also applies to the pet-slot effect chooser during the current app run. It starts unchecked after restarting or changing character.

Showing a charged effect does not establish that it can be converted or socketed. Included candidates carry a **transfer unverified** limitation. Existing source metadata, owned-effect evidence and saved assignments remain intact; hiding a candidate does not remove equipped or planned effects.
