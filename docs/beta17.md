# Beta 1.7

The translucent Catalyst toolbar opens Tracker, Combat Lab and the new read-only Overview. Each workspace keeps its own dimensions. Transparent navigation faces use a blue selected outline and gold hover brackets. Close and Settings stay transparent; the resize grip has its own space after Close. Player and pet context share the title row when space permits.

Overview follows Current or an independently selected historical session. It brings combat totals, loot, coin receipts and quest activity together without editing the underlying records. Context links deliberately open the relevant Tracker or Lab destination. Recorded progress is evidence, not automatic quest completion. Unknown income and incomplete history remain qualified.

Combat Lab retains the session-first Damage and Healing views, Filters beside Session, date limits in Tools and independent overlays. Pet changes remain drafts until Save; Undo restores the complete saved baseline. Save/Discard/Cancel protects navigation and shutdown. Pet Details separates observed snapshots, loadout additions and assumed standard buffs. No universal pet HP, mana or DPS formula is introduced.

Build compatibility, compact Bard song review, journal, notebook and holdings evidence are included. Bard evidence keeps caster, recipient, rank, instrument and Aura context separate. Unknown stacking or eligibility does not become a numerical upgrade.

Appearance offers independent Classic and class-inspired pet choices. Vibrant now uses the approved paintings at every size; Clean retains simplified vectors. Monster A is the default, with the approved interim Dragon/Aviak choices in Settings. Artwork never changes pet identity or attribution.

## Optional phone/browser sharing

Authenticated read-only sharing is included and **disabled by default**. Nothing listens until the user explicitly configures and enables it. Pairing and desktop approval are required; device permissions, revocation and character boundaries remain enforced. Browser access requires a matching trusted TLS certificate; certificate checks must not be bypassed. See [Phone and browser sharing](phone-companion.md).

Private Pet research stays disabled in public builds. No personal logs, profiles, research, pairing state or signing credentials belong in release packages. Windows binaries remain unsigned; the updater separately verifies signed update metadata and package hashes. No Android APK or Microsoft Store submission is included.
