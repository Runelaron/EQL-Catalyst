# Finding, tracking and prioritizing quests

Items and Quests are the discovery tabs. Queue, Progress, Gear, Exaltations and Pet follow them. Items continues to show quest collection requirements; changing tab order does not change saved goals.

**Track** includes a quest in Progress. **Untrack** stops tracking while preserving its recorded progress. **Add to queue** additionally pins a quest or item among prioritized goals. Removing a Queue pin does not untrack the quest. Both quest and item pins remain supported.

Quest and Progress searches include documented reward names and aliases. Progress shows the quest reward below Step status. Unknown reward metadata stays explicitly undocumented. Quest options contains the Route selector and source coverage; alternative routes do not create inventory ownership.

Expanders use a compact inset label. Click the arrow/label or use keyboard focus and Space to expand. Tooltip timing and All / Essential / None preferences remain unchanged.

Progress, quest checklists and prerequisites show an instruction once when its heading repeats the same wording. Whitespace and punctuation differences do not create a second copy. Distinct headings, canonical quest/step names and complete instructions remain preserved in the underlying quest data.

## Walkthrough and reward details

Step status and Reward now share one row. Select a reward to slide its read-only item facts over the right-hand walkthrough; Escape or an outside click returns to the walkthrough. See [Progress reward inspection](progress-rewards.md) for source cards, copy selection, motion and dismissal.
