# Local Pet research — focused validation

The prior comparison cards led with identical equipment scores despite different pet abilities. Cards now distinguish measured results, observed stat sheets and equipment-only points. No combat totals, encounter boundaries, ownership rules, rate denominator, custom weights or ranked-item calculation model were changed.

The identity detector previously required candidate activity within 60 seconds of a summon. It now retains pending evidence for up to ten minutes and requires a subsequent owned identity before late association. Failed attempts are rejected; manual and reviewed corrections are preserved. A shared resolver prevents an unresolved active pet from inheriting an older family icon. Live/session icons use the current or most recent owned pet; historical selections use time-ranged identity. Independent main/footer display choices are preserved.

Focused core checks cover idempotent backfill, conserved totals and time partitions, missing details, stat coverage and invalid HP, cast/landing/interruption distinction, spell-line versus upgrade/AA identity, missing fades, proc classification, context isolation, repeated-run/holdout/review gates, restart serialization, delayed ownership, failed summons and unresolved/historical icons. Existing summon and independent-selector regressions also pass. No broad regression or theme/DPI matrix was requested for this iteration.

Synthetic WPF smoke checks exercise local/public build gating, automatic revisioned collection, normal/minimum window widths, all three research tabs, reviewed stat entry, draft preservation on background arrival and measured-first cards. Imports are bounded to 16 MB, transactional through a copied state, and cannot approve models, identity corrections or overwrite combat totals. Public builds do not initialize or read private research storage. Publication uses immutable combat snapshots and rejects research work superseded by a newer evidence revision.

Personal screenshots, reviewed identities, encounter IDs and inspection values are imported only into private character research. They are absent from the bundled catalogs and synthetic previews. The local delivery records targeted privacy findings, verified payload hashes, retained release hashes and app/profile/research backup checks separately. Native game behavior and a universal haste/HP/mana/rank formula are not established by these checks.
