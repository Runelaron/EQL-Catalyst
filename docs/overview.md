# Overview

Overview is the third workspace on the shared toolbar. It summarizes a session without changing your loadout, quest progress or Combat Lab filters. Its Session choice is saved separately for each character. **Current** follows the latest retained session; choosing a dated session keeps it pinned.

The summary shows fights, summed combat time, Self damage/DPS in red, Pet damage/DPS in rose, and recorded effective healing in green. **Coin earned** combines recorded coin receipts and item sale proceeds; its tooltip shows the breakdown. **Coin spent** counts explicit logged purchase prices as whole transaction totals, including purchases of several items. Missing financial evidence remains **Unknown**, not zero or an inferred wallet change. Elapsed session time sits beside Combat details and includes time outside combat. **Session Ended/Last observed** is at the bottom right; last observed activity does not establish logout.

Loot and Quest progress sit beside each other in a wide window and stack in a narrow one. Open Evidence for receipt identities, sources and action context. Loot is acquisition history, not present ownership. Manual checklist actions remain separate from logged evidence and planned setup changes; they do not automatically complete quests.

Select a loot item for a read-only popup showing its recorded upgrade rank and shared item statistics. Escape, clicking outside or changing context dismisses it. Motion follows app and Windows preferences. Progress links open Tracker; **Combat details** opens Combat Lab with that session selected. Passive updates never change another selection.

**Upgrade Rating**, beside Loot, remembers your choice per character. Equipable items show signed **U / B** replacement gains: unbuffed and configured usual buffs. Both compare the same compatible position using Gear’s role, weights and eligibility against saved **Current player gear now**, rather than equipment at acquisition time. These are equipment preference points, not DPS predictions. Unknown rank, metadata or eligibility stays unavailable with an explanation. Browsing never saves drafts or changes inventory quantities.

Compact Evidence headers sit beneath Loot and Quest progress. Setup observations and Session conditions open from the bottom left into the existing scrolling content.

Tracker remains the place to manage goals, inventories and builds. Combat Lab retains encounter inspection, detailed metrics and configuration. Encounter History always shows encounter details; it is no longer replaced by a recap panel.

Drag the shared bar to move the attached workspace. Reorder all three workspace buttons by dragging, their Move left/right context commands or Alt+Left/Right. The toolbar and each workspace remember their sizes independently. Unsaved Tracker drafts still require Save, Discard or Cancel before switching.

## Song and source evidence

In Combat Lab’s Group view, Known members and Song coverage sit beside Recent groups. Song coverage opens **Loadout Comparison → Songs**, with detected/common sets and paired Keep/Swap results. Advanced Gem / Song / Rank / Keep review remains under Setup. Select a song to review its evidence; potency context is a separate disclosure. Search replacement songs rather than checking the entire catalog. Unknown ranks and modifiers remain unknown; recorded ranks above ten remain preserved. Save stays disabled until valid values change.

Aura selection retains the documented backward gem order, confirmed capacity and eligibility checks. Swaps remain conservative and require caster/recipient-qualified evidence; a cast attempt or a matching client effect slot alone does not prove coverage or stacking. The [Bard reference](https://eqlwiki.com/Bard) and source disagreements remain available in evidence.

Other-character holdings appear in one optional disclosure. Camp notes and their silent estimate use the same disclosure controls as the rest of Tracker. A saved note is personal evidence, not a spawn timer or drop-probability model.

Equipment preference points remain distinct from mechanical compatibility and measured DPS. A reviewed displayed speed ceiling applies only to its matching pet/equipment/buff context. It does not establish a universal cap or hidden overflow.

## Combat and equipment controls

**Max hit** is positive outgoing enemy damage within active filters. Genuine lifetap damage qualifies; healing, self and friendly targets do not. The ability appears beside the value; the tooltip identifies attacker, enemy target, damage type and time. Older summaries without sufficient outgoing-target evidence show unavailable. **Unknown** distinguishes anonymous damage from named actors with unconfirmed ownership or group membership; hover for attribution limits.

Pet’s Slot filter is beside Search; Expansion is inside Filter. Gear’s Filter contains Show, Compatibility, Rank and Rating. Both equipment searches are 170 DIP wide.
