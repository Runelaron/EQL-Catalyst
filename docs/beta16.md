# Beta 1.6

The shared translucent toolbar opens Tracker or Combat Lab. Drag its background to move the attached workspace; its buttons use the pointer cursor. The horizontal grip has its own space immediately left of Close. Toolbar width and each expanded workspace size remain separately saved. Double-click background space to toggle the last workspace. Buttons also offer keyboard Move left/right commands. Unsaved Pet work is protected before switching or closing.

## Sessions, Damage and Healing

Choose **Session → Current** to follow the latest retained login session. Choose an earlier date to keep that historical session fixed. Each entry contains its date, start time and fight count, which is not a count of confirmed kills. Hover for the complete label. **Stats** shows Session Ended; a final observed record does not prove logout. Live sessions show In progress.

Inspect an individual encounter through target/history selection, then choose **Back to session**. **Tools → Date range** applies the existing validated local-time limits within the selected session. Changing sessions clears encounter pins and dates. Damage and Healing share these encounter limits; their existing ability, actor, chart and target controls remain available.

**Filters** opens below the session row. **Zone** defaults to All zones and uses normalized zones recorded in that session. **Names only** keeps whole encounters containing an explicitly documented named NPC in its documented zone. Mixed fights retain all targets; target filtering can narrow them further. Unknown classifications are excluded and coverage is explained in Stats. Outside-combat healing cannot be attributed to a named encounter. Class correction evidence remains available through Tools; the retired Classes filter is removed. Overlay selections remain independent.

## Pet planning and appearance

Pet inventory edits remain drafts until **Save**; **Undo** restores the saved baseline. Both disable when unchanged. Save/Discard/Cancel protects build switches, workspace closure and shutdown. Numbered hand positions remain fixed; row drops replace or swap items, while configured Exaltations stay at numbered positions. A drop onto the candidate list removes that loadout item without changing player-inventory evidence.

**Pet Details** opens a character sheet with detected or explicitly overridden summon context. Rank and actual level are separate. Observed snapshots retain their conditions; equipment additions appear separately. **Added** means gear and active configured effects only. Resists (added) shows signed gear contributions, including +0; innate resists and unsupported HP/mana conversions stay unknown. **With standard buffs** is an assumed scenario learned from confirmed received effects, not a complete live buff roster. Burst effects remain separate. Equipment preference points are not measured DPS.

Weapon previews share the adopted 10% rank-damage model and preserve exact recorded exceptions, fixed delay and known evidence. Catalog intrinsic Exaltations require explicit selection; conflicting saved effect types remain reviewable and inactive. Current and planned summon settings remain independent. Monster, the October content identities, race level and incomplete mechanics remain explicitly qualified.

In **Settings → Appearance**, Fire defaults to **Wizard staff**, with **Wizard crystal** as its alternative. Undead independently offers Skeleton A, Wraith A or Wraith B. Choices are per character and apply immediately. Clean and small (24 DIP or below) Vibrant controls use simplified art; larger Vibrant controls use paintings decoded for display density. Artwork never changes detected identity or combat attribution.

The [round-four gallery](previews/pet-art-r4-review.html) contains seven review-only revisions, archived earlier concepts and actual DPS-control captures. Air, Water, Warder and Monster retain their selected production art until another selection is approved.

## Catalog coverage

The October 23 refresh checks all 18,989 indexed item titles against revisions, fetches 124 new titles and 142 changed bundled definitions, and follows Nektropos, Lake Nerius and Undercroft backlinks and related pages. Bundled unique item definitions increase from 6,808 to 6,906. Seven partial quest walkthroughs retain their missing steps. Acquisition gaps decrease from 2,639 to 2,556. The explicit named-NPC index has 923 zone-qualified records; 6,053 other zone records remain unclassified.

Blank Itempage examples are not item statistics. Relationships can remain useful when numbers are unknown. Related-quest links alone do not establish rewards. Source URLs, revisions, timestamps and disagreements remain in offline evidence. EQLWiki-derived material is CC BY-SA 4.0. EQL Tools attributes its gear data to EQLWiki and is not independent confirmation. Official content/access announcements do not supply unpublished item or pet formulas. Full per-title dispositions and unresolved acquisition identities are in `data/content-coverage.json`.

Private Pet research remains disabled in public builds. Public bundles contain synthetic examples and documented catalogs, not personal logs, screenshots, inventories or research. Capture remains opt-in. No game file writes, new input automation or profile migration is introduced.
