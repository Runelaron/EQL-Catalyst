Shared workspace toolbar

A fixed 28-DIP gold-branded bar opens Tracker or Combat Lab. Click the selected workspace to close it, or click the other to switch. Outside clicks leave the workspace selected. Drag the bar to move the attached panel; use the right grip to resize the bar horizontally.

Buttons can be dragged to reorder. Their context menus also offer Move left / Move right; Alt+Left/Right works from a focused button. Double-click empty bar space to toggle the last workspace. Settings is icon-only; a ready update appears immediately to its right.
