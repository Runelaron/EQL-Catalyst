# Pet comparison and planning

Open **Pet → Tools → Compare pets**. Choose eligible summons and inspect measured context before comparing results. Current follows confirmed owned summons unless its build assumptions are explicitly overridden. Planned builds retain independent choices; spell rank and observed pet level remain separate.

Measured DPS, observed HP/AC, sample duration/count and conditions lead the cards when matching evidence exists. Unmatched captures remain historical. Equipment damage/defense points live in details and score the equipment, not innate pet abilities. Equal gear may have equal points while pets have different observed results. No universal DPS, STA→HP or INT/WIS→mana coefficient is supplied.

Inventory, rank, effects, Dual wield and editable summon assumptions are drafts. **Save** commits the baseline; **Undo** restores it. Leaving unsaved work offers Save/Discard/Cancel. Theorycraft retains its separate naming/designation requirements. Tools with their own Save retain that action.

Position 1 is Primary; position 2 is Secondary when Dual wield is enabled. Two-handed equipment makes Secondary inactive. Drops onto a row replace it; pet-row drops swap item identities/ranks while Exaltations stay at numbered positions. Dropping back onto candidates removes that loadout item without manufacturing player-inventory evidence. Incompatible and conflicting effects remain visible for review. Catalog intrinsic effects require explicit selection.

Rank previews use the shared adopted damage model, `floor(base damage × (1 + 0.10 × rank))`, with exact recorded exceptions retained. Fixed delay/accuracy and ordinary-stat rules remain separate. Formula disagreements stay in evidence. Previewing never changes recorded rank, ownership or saved effects.

**Pet Details** shows a compact observed character sheet with capture conditions, loadout weapons and separate gear contributions. **Added** excludes received buffs. **With standard buffs** is a labeled scenario using confirmed learned sustained effects; it does not assert a complete live roster. Frenzied Burnout remains a burst effect. Unknown innate resists and unsupported conversions remain unknown.

See [Beta 1.6 help](beta16.md), [measurement process](pet-measurement-guide.md) and [connected spell evidence](connected-spell-evidence.md). Private research is disabled in public builds; profiles and personal captures are not bundled.
