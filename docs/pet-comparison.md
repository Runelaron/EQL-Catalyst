# Pet comparison and live item evidence

Open **Pet → Compare pets…**. Each pet has a compact card with its type, attainable level, DPS, HP and separate changes from Current. **Unknown** means the required evidence is missing; it does not mean zero.

The normal comparison keeps the same effective equipment as the Pet tab. Explicit intended assignments stay authoritative. Current's existing inferred assignments are carried across consistently and remain labeled as inferred. A pet that cannot attain the shared level shows its attainable level. A different pet's incompatible items do not contribute.

## Equipment previews

**Optimized gear** starts off. **Inventory** considers recorded equippable copies at their recorded ranks, including documented Pet inventory. Required player-storage or worn-item transfers are listed. Matching stale player/Pet evidence overlaps conservatively; components, collection presence and recipes do not create equipment copies.

**Best Possible** considers compatible catalog equipment at +10 within the comparison's own Expansion filter, initially Classic. Kunark and Velious are explicitly future scenarios. These assignments are hypothetical and are saved as Theorycraft. A bounded preference search considers both weapon configurations and strongest worn haste; it does not establish optimal pet DPS. Balanced retains separate damage/defense tradeoffs.

**Rank-efficient inventory upgrades** offers Selected item or Whole build. It shows up to five supported preference improvements ordered by the fewest additional ranks required, through +10. It identifies the owned rank, required rank and item replaced. No items are upgraded or transferred automatically. Unsupported offhand mechanics can prevent a confirmed upgrade result.

**Save as Pet plan…** creates a new named plan. It never changes Current or creates inventory ownership. Missing learning, unverified buffs, future equipment and unsupported assumptions retain their evidence and Theorycraft designation.

## Goals, weights and actual performance

DPS and Tank use independent editable equipment priorities. Balanced keeps both dimensions rather than inventing a combined score. Expand Weights & arithmetic to inspect the actual weights. Preference points are not DPS, HP, proc rates or damage reduction.

The bundled definitions do not contain a complete verified EQL pet damage or survival model. Unknown innate baselines, cadence, target response and casting interference remain unknown. Item ratios are not converted into a universal damage formula. The [October 13 pet changes](https://www.everquestlegends.com/patch-notes/eql-update-notes-10-13-2026) version the weapon/level mechanics; [EQL Tools' sources](https://eqltools.com/sources) explain limits of client-derived stacking evidence.

Expand the selected pet's details to **Record benchmark…**. Enter measured DPS/HP or select finished encounters. Encounter DPS is total pet damage divided by summed encounter duration. Review the level, ranks, equipment, buffs, positioning and targets before confirming the test context. Unconfirmed samples stay historical; confirmed values populate only matching-context cards and are labeled measured. Other pet families and changed mechanics never inherit those numbers.

Summon and timestamp-qualified ownership evidence identify a pet. Target names and individual casts alone do not prove its identity. Repeat comparable targets when testing equipment; record swaps and buff conditions explicitly. Samples with different gear, ranks, targets or buff coverage do not establish an isolated offhand multiplier.

## Live item updates

Loot, merge observations, complete exports and arriving metadata refresh affected item, quest, Gear and Pet views. Standalone merge messages update a rank only when one recorded physical copy can be identified. Ambiguous copies retain their ranks and an unresolved observation until a complete export or correction establishes the facts. Observed loot never establishes worn equipment.

Newly acquired documented components track active-class quests while respecting explicit untracking, completion and planning expansions. **Tracked / All** controls Progress browsing; All does not add everything to your Queue.

In Gear and Pet candidates, **Default** uses recorded physical ranks or catalog +0. Explicit +0–10 previews every candidate, including owned copies. The selected-item slider remains independent. Manual inventory additions keep physical-instance ranks or catalog +0. Previewing an upgrade never creates ownership of it.

Default also retains recorded manual Pet inventory ranks when the player snapshot no longer contains a transferred copy. This does not create player ownership evidence. Matching stale player/Pet evidence is conservatively overlapped. See [ranked-stat evidence](pet-rating-evidence.md) for observed values, Conditional estimates and the corrected Whip/Evensong example.

**Obtain From** distinguishes recorded inventory possession, quest/start locations, drops and other documented routes. Unknown quest starting locations remain unknown. EQLWiki-derived fields retain revision attribution and the existing CC BY-SA notices.


## Inventory positions and Dual wield

Position 1 is the Primary planning position. Dual wield additionally reserves position 2 for Secondary. Turning Dual wield off keeps its weapon and effects as dormant configuration and frees position 2. Re-enabling cannot overwrite armor; free capacity first. Armor compaction preserves entries and order. Two-handed equipment disables effective Secondary without deleting configuration.

Configured Exaltations stay with numbered positions when equipment is moved or replaced. Intrinsic item effects follow the item. A retained effect on another item is planned intent, not evidence of a physical transfer or socketing. Rank, class, slot and activation restrictions still apply; incompatible effects remain visible and inactive. Double-click an inventory name or Procs cell to edit its position, including empty positions. Ordinary builds autosave; Theorycraft still requires explicit saving.

Weapon selection follows documented inventory ordering where supported. A Primary-only weapon placed after a flexible Primary/Secondary weapon is not automatically swapped into Primary. Shield replacement observations and competing two-handers remain Conditional. The planner does not infer a game selection rule from the sum of two weapon ratios.

Pet cards appear side by side, with independent DPS and HP changes versus Current. Missing supported predictions remain Unknown. Inventory/effect edits refresh the candidate baseline and open comparison; optimization remains an explicit preview.

## Computed equipment cards

Damage preference, Defense preference and Gear HP now lead each card, with separate Current deltas. Same compatible gear may yield equal points across families; intrinsic DPS is a separate, evidence-dependent result. Gear HP is equipment HP only. See [Compact layout and coverage](compact-pet-layout.md).
